What we do

Our activities

Apex Residential Solutions Ltd is registered at Companies House for two areas of property activity.

Buying and selling property

We buy and sell real estate on our own account, as recorded under SIC code 68100 (buying and selling of own real estate).

Letting and operating property

We let and operate real estate that we own or lease, as recorded under SIC code 68209 (other letting and operating of own or leased real estate).
